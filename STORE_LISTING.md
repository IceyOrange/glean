# Chrome Web Store Listing — Glean

## Short Description (132 chars max)

Clip quotes from any webpage, jot down your thoughts, and let AI find connections — one card at a time.

## Detailed Description

### 🖊️ Clip What Moves You

Glean is a quiet, distraction-free browser extension for saving the lines that strike you while reading the web. Select any text, click the floating quote icon, and it's saved as a card — with room for your own thoughts right beside it.

### ✨ Features

**One-Click Clipping**
Select text on any webpage and click the floating quote icon. The selected text, source page title, and URL are saved instantly as a card.

**Instant Annotations**
Write your thoughts right after clipping. Press Shift + Enter for a new line. Your annotation lives alongside the quote, always in context.

**AI Insights**
Connect to your preferred LLM (DeepSeek, OpenAI, Anthropic, Moonshot, SiliconFlow, Zhipu/BigModel, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint) to discover patterns, connections, and new ideas across your clippings. You bring the API key — Glean does the rest.

**History Journal**
A dedicated full-page view to browse, search, edit, bulk-delete, or export all your cards. Filter by date, keyword, or source. Your personal library of ideas, always at hand.

**Cloud Sync**
Sync your clippings to a Notion database or back up via WebDAV / Nutstore (坚果云). Your data, your cloud.

**Dark Mode**
Follows your system preference, or toggle manually. Easy on the eyes, day or night.

**Multilingual**
Built-in support for English, 中文, and Français.

### 🔒 Privacy First

- All clippings are stored **locally** on your device by default.
- No data is sent anywhere unless you explicitly configure a third-party service.
- API keys and tokens are stored locally and only used for the services you set up.
- Glean is fully open-source — inspect the code at https://github.com/IceyOrange/glean

### 📋 Why These Permissions?

- **Read and change all data on websites you visit** — Glean needs to detect text selections on any page so you can clip quotes from anywhere. No page content is read unless you actively select text and click the clip button.
- **Read your browsing history** — Used to record the source page URL and title when you save a quote, providing context for each card.
- **Storage** — To save your clippings, annotations, and settings locally on your device.
- **Alarms** — To schedule periodic background sync tasks when you enable cloud sync.

# Chrome Web Store 中文版

## 简短描述（132字符以内）

划词摘录网页金句，随手记下灵感，让 AI 发现思维之间的联系——一次一张卡片。

## 详细描述

### 🖊️ 摘录打动你的文字

Glean 是一款安静、无干扰的浏览器扩展，专为保存阅读中打动你的句子而设计。选中任意文字，点击浮现的引号图标，即可保存为卡片——旁边还有空间写下你的想法。

### ✨ 功能

**划词摘录**
在任意网页选中文字，点击浮现的引号图标，选中的文字、来源页面标题和链接即刻保存为卡片。

**即时批注**
摘录后立刻写下想法，按 Shift + Enter 换行。批注与引文并列，始终在上下文中。

**AI 洞察**
接入你常用的大语言模型（DeepSeek、OpenAI、Anthropic、Moonshot、SiliconFlow、智谱/BigModel，或任何 OpenAI 兼容接口），发现摘录之间的思维模式和灵感联系。你提供 API Key，Glean 负责其余。

**灵感库**
独立的 Journal 全屏页面，可浏览、搜索、编辑、批量删除或导出所有卡片。按日期、关键词或来源筛选——你的个人灵感库，随时可用。

**云同步**
将摘录同步到 Notion 数据库，或通过 WebDAV / 坚果云备份。你的数据，你的云端。

**暗色模式**
跟随系统偏好，或手动切换。白天黑夜，护眼舒适。

**多语言**
内置中文、英文、法文界面。

### 🔒 隐私优先

- 所有摘录默认**本地存储**在你的设备上。
- 未经你主动配置第三方服务，不会向任何地方发送数据。
- API 密钥和令牌仅保存在本地，仅用于你设置的服务。
- Glean 完全开源——在 https://github.com/IceyOrange/glean 查看源码。

### 📋 为什么需要这些权限？

- **读取和更改您访问的所有网站上的数据** — Glean 需要在任意网页检测文字选中，以便你随时摘录。除非你主动选中文字并点击摘录按钮，否则不会读取任何页面内容。
- **读取您的浏览记录** — 用于在保存摘录时记录来源页面的 URL 和标题，为每张卡片提供上下文。
- **存储** — 在你的设备上本地保存摘录、批注和设置。
- **闹钟** — 在你启用云同步后，用于调度定期的后台同步任务。
